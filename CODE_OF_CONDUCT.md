# Community conduct

Treat learners, educators and contributors with dignity. Welcome different abilities, languages, cultures, genders and levels of experience. Give concrete, respectful feedback and distinguish evidence from interpretation. Harassment, discrimination, coercion, doxxing, exploitation, and fabricated credentials or endorsements are unacceptable.

Do not post private information or safeguarding reports in public issues. Use GitHub's private abuse reporting tools for abuse on the platform. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate public contributions, restrict participation, and document decisions without disclosing affected people's private information.

This repository is a collaboration space, not an emergency or safeguarding reporting service. Each adopting community must establish its own accessible, trusted local reporting and response process.
