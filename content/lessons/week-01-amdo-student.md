🏔️ W01 L1-L4: AMDO-FACING STUDENT WORKSHEET & ACTIVITIES

Student Instructions & Inquiry Prompts
* Objective: Master basic self-introduction, classroom object identification, and bilingual proverbs bridging Amdo, Lhasa, and US English.

---

Activity 1: The Tri-Dialectal Greeting Pass
* Task: Pair up with a peer. Practice greeting each other in Amdo phonetics and US English.
   1. Amdo: ཁྱེད་རང་སྐུ་ཁམས་བཟང་། (Khyed-rang sku-kham bzang!)
   2. English: "Hello! How are you today?"
   3. Response: "I am wonderful, thank you."
Activity 2: Object Matching Challenge
* Task: Draw a line from the English word to the Tibetan Uchen script and Amdo pronunciation:
   * Water → ཆུ། → Chy
   * Book → དཔེ་ཆ། → Hpe-cha
   * Horse → རྟ། → Rta
Activity 3: Proverb & Wordplay Inquiry
* Proverb: ཁ་བཤད་ཆུ་ཡི་ལྦུ་བ། ལག་ལེན་གསེར་གྱི་ཐིགས་པ།
* Question 1: What does a water bubble do when you touch it? What does gold do?
* Question 2: Write down one way you can show "drops of gold" (actions) in your school this week instead of "water bubbles" (empty words).
