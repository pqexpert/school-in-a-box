# Week 1: Identity — Amdo teacher

🏔️ W01 L1-L4: AMDO-FACING TEACHER MASTER GUIDE

I. Pedagogical Facilitation Notes & Embedded Answer Keys
* Target Audience: Educators operating across Boys K-12, Girls K-12, and Monastic Seminars.
* Linguistic Rule: Always bridge Amdo phonetics (Chy, Rta) to Lhasa standard (Chu, Ta) before introducing US English equivalents (Water, Horse).

---

II. Module Breakdown & Answer Key Matrix
1. Level 1 (Seed / Sa-bon) — Survival Greetings & Classroom Objects
* Teacher Prompt (Amdo): ཁྱེད་རང་སྐུ་ཁམས་བཟང་། ངའི་མིང་ལ་...ཟེར། (Khyed-rang sku-kham bzang! Nga'i ming la... zer.)
* Student Expected Response (English/Amdo): "Hello! My name is..."
* Embedded Answer Key & Misconception Correction:
   * Common Error: Students confuse English "What is your name?" with "Where are you going?".
   * Teacher Correction: Model pointing to oneself ("Nga" / I) versus pointing to the student ("Khyed-rang" / You).
   * Zero-Tech Backup: Point to physical classroom objects (Pe-cha / Book, Chy / Water) and have students chorus the English noun three times.
2. Level 2 (Sprout / Myu-gu) — Family & Courtyard Mapping
* Teacher Prompt (Amdo): ང་ཚོའི་ཁྱིམ་ཚང་དང་སློབ་གྲྭ་ནི་རྩྭ་ཐང་ན་ཡོད། (Nga-tsho'i khyim-tsang dang slob-grwa ni rtswa-thang na yod.)
* Embedded Answer Key (Translated Dialogue):
   * Q: Where is your home? -> A: My home is on the grassland near the river. (Nga'i khyim-tsang rtswa-thang dang chu-bo'i nye-da na yod.)
   * Embedded Rubric Check: Student must use a complete sentence with subject, location verb (yod), and preposition.
3. Level 3 (Lotus / Pad-ma) — Rig-lam Logic & Proverb Analysis
* Proverb Focus: ཁ་བཤད་ཆུ་ཡི་ལྦུ་བ། ལག་ལེན་གསེར་གྱི་ཐིགས་པ། (Words are water bubbles; actions are drops of gold.)
* Embedded Answer Key (Philosophical & Linguistic Analysis):
   * Literal Meaning: Speech vanishes quickly like a bubble; deeds remain valuable like gold.
   * English Idiom Bridge: "Actions speak louder than words."
   * Rig-lam Debate Prompt: "Is speech without action useful?" -> Expected Student Answer: "No, just as a water bubble cannot quench thirst, words without action cannot build a school."
4. Level 4 — Peer teaching and reflection
* Prepare a short bilingual explanation of this week’s theme.
* Invite a partner to teach it back, then compare clarity and respectful language.
* Teacher rubric: clear meaning, understandable phrasing, and thoughtful peer feedback.
* Optional cultural or religious extensions belong to the local community and require approved sources and teacher review.
