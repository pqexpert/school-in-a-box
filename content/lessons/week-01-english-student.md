🏔️ W01 L1-L4: ENGLISH-FACING STUDENT WORKSHEET & ACTIVITIES

Student Learning Guide & Exercises
* Core Mission: Develop bilingual English-Tibetan fluency, global 21st-century skills, and rigorous philosophical inquiry.

---

Activity 1: The English Introduction Passport
* Task: Fill in the blanks and practice reading aloud with your partner:
   * "Hello, my name is ---_______."
   * "I am studying at the our school in ---_______."
   * "My favorite subject is ---_______."
Activity 2: Vocabulary Matching & Sentence Building
* Task: Match the English term on the left with its Amdo pronunciation and Tibetan script:
   1. Water → [  ] → ཆུ། (Chy)
   2. Teacher → [  ] → དགེ་རྒན། (Ge-rgan)
   3. Wisdom → [  ] → ཤེས་རབ། (She-rab)
Activity 3: Critical Thinking & Growth Mindset Reflection
* Prompt: Write three sentences in English about something you found difficult to learn and how practice or a partner helped you improve.
