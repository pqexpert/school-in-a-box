I. Educator Facilitation & Washington D.C. / Tibet Bridge Notes
* Pedagogical Alignment: Constructivist inquiry combined with rigorous Amdo-Lhasa-English linguistic scaffolding.
* Embedded Answer Key & Rubric Guidance:
   * Warm-Up Assessment: Check whether students can transition between Amdo phonetics (Chy) and Lhasa standard (Chu).
   * Formative Threshold: 75% of students must successfully execute the 5-object touch test during Gate 1. If below 75%, insert a 20-minute kinesthetic TPR review block on Tuesday.

---

II. Module Objectives & Answer Key Matrix
1. Level 1 (Seed / Sa-bon)
* Objective: Master basic greetings and singular/plural nouns.
* Answer Key: "Hello" = ཁྱེད་རང་སྐུ་ཁམས་བཟང་།; "Book" = དཔེ་ཆ། (Pe-cha).
2. Level 2 (Sprout / Myu-gu)
* Objective: Construct compound sentences describing home environment.
* Answer Key: Sentence frame: "My home is located on the grassland where the river flows."
3. Level 3 (Lotus / Pad-ma)
* Objective: Compare Western idioms with Amdo proverbs.
* Answer Key: Actions speak louder than words matches ཁ་བཤད་ཆུ་ཡི་ལྦུ་བ། ལག་ལེན་གསེར་གྱི་ཐིགས་པ།.
4. Level 4 — Peer teaching and reflection
* Prepare a short bilingual explanation of this week’s theme.
* Invite a partner to teach it back, then compare clarity and respectful language.
* Teacher rubric: clear meaning, understandable phrasing, and thoughtful peer feedback.
* Optional cultural or religious extensions belong to the local community and require approved sources and teacher review.
