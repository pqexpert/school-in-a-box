🏔️ W02 L1-L4: AMDO-FACING STUDENT WORKSHEET & ACTIVITIES

Student Instructions & Inquiry Prompts
* Objective: Practice hospitality dialogues, tea-serving etiquette, and cultural protocol across Amdo, Lhasa, and US English.

---

Activity 1: The Butter-Tea Serving Roleplay
* Task: Pair with a classmate. One acts as the host offering tea (Zhes-ja); the other acts as the guest accepting with gratitude (Thugs-rje che).
   1. Host: བཞེས་ཇ་ཕུལ། (Zhes-ja phul!)
   2. Guest: ཐུགས་རྗེ་ཆེ། ལགས་སོ། (Thugs-rje che! Lags-so.)
Activity 2: Vocabulary & Honorific Matching
* Task: Match the standard term with its honorific (Zhe-sa) form:
   * Drink → བཞེས་པ (Zhes-pa)
   * Eat → མཆོད་པ (Mchod-pa)
   * Speak → གསུང་པ (Gsung-pa)
Activity 3: Cross-Cultural Diplomacy Reflection
* Prompt: Compare how guests are welcomed in Washington, D.C. versus a traditional Amdo pasture tent or monastery. Write 2 sentences in English describing why hospitality is the foundation of global peace.
