# Week 2: Hospitality — Amdo teacher

🏔️ W02 L1-L4: AMDO-FACING TEACHER MASTER GUIDE

I. Pedagogical Facilitation Notes & Embedded Answer Keys
* Target Audience: Educators operating across Boys K-12, Girls K-12, and Monastic Seminars.
* Theme: Hospitality, Guest Etiquette, and Diplomatic Protocol (Mgron-bsu).
* Linguistic Focus: Bridging Amdo honorific speech with Lhasa standard honorifics (Zhe-sa) and US diplomatic small talk.

---

II. Module Breakdown & Answer Key Matrix
1. Level 1 (Seed / Sa-bon) — Serving Tea & Basic Politeness
* Teacher Prompt (Amdo): བཞེས་ཇ་ཕུལ། ཐུགས་རྗེ་ཆེ། (Zhes-ja phul! Thugs-rje che.)
* Embedded Answer Key & Misconception Correction:
   * Translation: "Please accept the tea! Thank you."
   * Common Error: Using informal verbs when offering tea to elders or monks.
   * Teacher Correction: Model honorific verb usage (Zhes) and bowing posture.
2. Level 2 (Sprout / Myu-gu) — Hosting an American Guest in Tibet
* Teacher Prompt (Amdo): ཕེབས་པ་དགའ་བསུ་ཞུ། ཁྱེད་རང་སྐུ་ཁམས་བཟང་ངམ། (Phebs-pa dga'-bsu zhu! Khyed-rang sku-kham bzang-ngam?)
* Embedded Answer Key (Dialogue Response):
   * Q: "Welcome! Are you in good health?" -> A: "Yes, thank you for your wonderful hospitality." (* lags-so, thugs-rje che.*)
3. Level 3 (Lotus / Pad-ma) — Rig-lam Logic & Hospitality Proverbs
* Proverb Focus: མགྲོན་པོ་ལ་བརྩི་མཐོང་། བདག་པོ་ལ་ཞབས་ཏོག (Respect for the guest; service to the host.)
* Embedded Answer Key & Philosophical Analysis:
   * Discussion Prompt: How does hosting reflect the core constructivist value of mutual respect?
   * Rubric Answer: Hosting bridges cultural boundaries by recognizing the shared humanity and dignity of every traveler.
4. Level 4 — Peer teaching and reflection
* Prepare a short bilingual explanation of this week’s theme.
* Invite a partner to teach it back, then compare clarity and respectful language.
* Teacher rubric: clear meaning, understandable phrasing, and thoughtful peer feedback.
* Optional cultural or religious extensions belong to the local community and require approved sources and teacher review.
