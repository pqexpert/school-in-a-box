Student Learning Guide & Exercises

Activity 1: The Diplomatic Greeting Dialogue
* Task: Fill in the missing English and Tibetan phrases for welcoming a guest to your campus:
   * "Welcome to our school! ---_______ (Phebs-pa dga'-bsu zhu!)"
   * "Please have some butter tea. ---_______ (Zhes-ja phul!)"
   * "Thank you very much. ---_______ (Thugs-rje che!)"
Activity 2: Vocabulary & Concept Matching
* Task: Match the concept on the left with its description on the right:
   1. Mgron-bsu → [  ] Respect for the guest and hosting etiquette
   2. Zhe-sa → [  ] Honorific language used for elders and honored guests
   3. Snying-rje → [  ] Universal compassion and empathy
Activity 3: Cross-Cultural Writing Prompt
* Prompt: Write a short paragraph in English explaining how treating a guest with respect in Amdo Tibetan culture connects to the 21st-century global skill of cross-cultural empathy.
