I. Educator Facilitation & Washington D.C. / Tibet Bridge Notes
* Pedagogical Alignment: Cultivating cross-cultural empathy and diplomatic grace through hospitality protocols.
* Embedded Answer Key & Rubric Guidance:
   * Assessment Target: Students must demonstrate correct honorific usage and polite conversational exchanges in both Amdo Tibetan and English.
   * Formative Threshold: 80% accuracy in pairing honorific verbs with respectful guest interactions.

---

II. Module Objectives & Answer Key Matrix
1. Level 1 (Seed / Sa-bon)
* Objective: Master serving and greeting vocabulary.
* Answer Key: "Please accept the tea" = བཞེས་ཇ་ཕུལ།; "Thank you" = ཐུགས་རྗེ་ཆེ།.
2. Level 2 (Sprout / Myu-gu)
* Objective: Execute welcoming dialogues for visitors.
* Answer Key: Sentence frame: "Welcome to our campus! We are honored by your presence."
3. Level 3 (Lotus / Pad-ma)
* Objective: Analyze proverbs on hospitality.
* Answer Key: Respect for the guest; service to the host emphasizes reciprocity in community building.
4. Level 4 — Peer teaching and reflection
* Prepare a short bilingual explanation of this week’s theme.
* Invite a partner to teach it back, then compare clarity and respectful language.
* Teacher rubric: clear meaning, understandable phrasing, and thoughtful peer feedback.
* Optional cultural or religious extensions belong to the local community and require approved sources and teacher review.
