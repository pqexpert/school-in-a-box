- Local process for consent, concerns and a safe departure: ______

Keep real names and contact details in the community's appropriate private process, not in public issues or portal notes.

## Shared learning rhythm

Welcome and check-in → Demonstration → Partner practice → Inquiry → Teach-back → Reflection.

Example 60-minute session: 5 minutes welcome, 10 demonstration, 15 pair practice, 15 inquiry, 10 teach-back, 5 reflection. Adjust timing for age, access, fatigue and local commitments; this is a planning example.

## Equal opportunity and stewardship

Use the same learning objectives and opportunity to participate across campuses. Compare aggregate availability of books, devices, printouts, teacher time and accessible spaces. Identify barriers with the people affected. Do not infer ability, attendance or entitlement from a campus label or gender.

## Decisions remain human

Who can change the timetable? Who reviews language and cultural content? Who stops a session if conditions become unsafe? Agree and document these locally. This app has no authority to assign people, spend money, decide compliance or operate a campus.
