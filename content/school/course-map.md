# Amdo Tibetan ↔ English course map

Draft course design · CC BY 4.0

## Available course units

- Week 1: Identity — greetings, introductions, classroom objects and communicating through action.
- Week 2: Hospitality — welcoming a guest, polite dialogue, respectful language and reflection.

Each unit has Amdo-facing and English-facing student activities and teacher guides with embedded answers. Documents combine four levels. The portal's level filter finds documents tagged for a level; it does not rewrite lessons or assess the learner.

## Four levels as flexible scaffolding

- L1 Seed: notice objects, respond through gesture, and practice short phrases.
- L2 Sprout: form sentences, ask questions, and take turns in dialogue.
- L3 Lotus: compare meanings, explain choices, and investigate with a partner.
- L4 Vajra: teach a concept to someone else, invite feedback, and revise the explanation.

These are course labels, not age, status or spiritual attainment. Let educators and learners choose suitable scaffolding. Access to the same resources remains open at every level.

## Review before teaching

Fluent local educators should verify dialect, spelling, register, pronunciation, translations and answer guides. Distinguish regional variation from an error. Religious or philosophical extensions are optional and require local approval, verified sources and permission to share; the public starter pack does not reproduce a religious curriculum.

## Expansion boundary

Weeks 3–8 and the longer 36-week academic-year concept are planned work. No lesson, certificate, attendance record, school approval or learning outcome is implied by the course map. Expand only as reviewed material becomes available.
