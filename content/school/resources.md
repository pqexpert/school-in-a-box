# Resource and offline readiness checklist

Local preparation template · CC BY 4.0

## Before a session

- A trusted, accessible place and an appropriate human facilitator.
- Educator-reviewed student and teacher materials.
- One computer with Python 3.11+ if using the portal; paper or slate alternatives for everyone.
- Power and readable Tibetan font rendering checked on the actual device.
- Downloaded release and matching SHA-256 checksum; a backup copy on USB if useful.
- Printing checked while internet is disconnected.
- Browser-local notes exported before changing shared profiles or devices.

## Shared resources

Use a local sheet for item type, number available, number needed, accessible alternative and person responsible. Avoid learner names. Compare access across campus contexts before acquiring more devices. Software is freely reusable; hardware, electricity, printing and local support may have costs.

## Keep the deployment small

An existing laptop and printed sheets may be sufficient. LAN hosting is optional and needs an authorized local administrator. This kit does not configure a captive portal, manage network access, provide emergency support, or monitor health. Local safety and emergency procedures belong with qualified responsible people.
