# Educator and community review

Review template · CC BY 4.0

Document/week/version: ______  Scope of review: ______

## Language

- Is the Tibetan text readable and spelled appropriately for its intended use?
- Are dialect, transliteration, pronunciation, register and English meaning distinguished?
- Does the teacher guide actually answer the student task?
- Are variants acknowledged rather than presented as one universal pronunciation?

## Teaching and access

- Is the learner task clear, age-appropriate and possible with available resources?
- Can learners participate without a device, and in accessible modes?
- Does peer teaching support understanding without public ranking or coercion?
- Are examples respectful and free of gender stereotypes or assumed religious participation?

## Sources and local context

- Are quotations verifiable, accurately attributed and permitted for redistribution?
- Are any cultural or religious extensions voluntary and locally approved?
- Has the community decided its own responsibilities, safeguarding and consent processes?
- Does the plan avoid claiming accreditation, compliance or a running campus without evidence?

Record proposed corrections and any limits on the review. Name a reviewer publicly only with consent. Reviewing one document does not validate the whole course, software or school system.
