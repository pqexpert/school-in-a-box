# Set up your school in a box

An adaptable planning guide · CC BY 4.0

## 1. Start with the people

Ask learners, educators and community representatives what learning would help them. Agree on language, accessibility needs, voluntary participation and who is responsible for a safe session. Establish a trusted local safeguarding and consent process before running activities with children. Keep personal information out of this public project and portal notes.

## 2. Open the box

Download a release, extract it, and run `python3 server.py` with Python 3.11+. Open http://127.0.0.1:8080. Read and print a student worksheet and its matching teacher guide. Disconnect internet access and confirm the local library still works. Keep printed copies if power or devices are unavailable.

## 3. Choose a context

Use the community classroom template or adapt a DABA campus context. Every campus receives the same course resources. Labels do not establish an institution or impose a gender or religious identity. Local people decide suitable schedules and optional cultural content.

## 4. Plan one useful session

Choose Week 1: Identity. Agree on a small learning objective. A teacher checks the language first, learners practice with a partner, and each partner teaches back something learned. Use the teaching-plan template to prepare an offline alternative. Introduce Week 2 only when the group is ready.

## 5. Learn and improve

Invite learners to explain what helped and what was confusing. Adjust the plan through educator judgment. Avoid automatic placement, promotion or remediation based on a group score. Share only non-identifying observations and openly licensed improvements upstream.

The portal is a resource library and planning kit. Its successful startup does not establish school accreditation, legal compliance, campus readiness or a complete curriculum.
