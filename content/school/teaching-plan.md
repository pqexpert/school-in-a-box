## Practice

1. Show one example using a familiar physical object or situation.
2. Let partners practice and change roles.
3. Invite each learner to explain a small part to the partner.
4. Ask the partner to demonstrate or explain what they understood.
5. Offer specific feedback; let learners revise without public ranking.

## Evidence and reflection

What could the group demonstrate? What needs another explanation? Which language points remain uncertain? Record only non-identifying teaching observations here. Use educator judgment for next steps; the app does not grade or promote anyone.

## No-device fallback

Read or demonstrate the same prompt aloud. Use a slate, paper, physical objects or a courtyard conversation. Keep the objective and feedback opportunity equivalent. Do not require a device or internet connection for participation.
