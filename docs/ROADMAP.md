# Roadmap and acceptance

## v0.1 — Available starter kit

- Read-only offline portal; two-week Amdo–English course; eight lesson files.
- Four level tags, teacher/student collections, print layouts, personal notes import/export.
- Campus planning templates and editable course metadata.
- Reproducible catalog/static build, standard-library tests and downloadable source release.

## Next — Educator-reviewed pilot

- Fluent Amdo educators review spelling, dialect, pronunciation and register. Record review per lesson, not as a blanket certificate.
- Educators check activities, inclusion, answers, assessment and classroom usability with community input.
- Trial one supervised lesson and the printable fallback in an appropriate local context, with necessary permissions. Record aggregate usability observations without student data.
- Improve screen-reader navigation, Tibetan font rendering and low-resource device use based on actual testing.

## Course expansion

- Develop weeks 3–8 only after the initial scope is reviewed. Each week needs both language facings and separate activity/teacher guides, provenance, level objectives and an offline alternative.
- Extend toward a 36-week course through reviewed contributions. No placeholder is presented as an available lesson.
- Add genuinely open course packs and translations through the same content interface.

## Later, only with demonstrated need

Optional audio with speaker consent and redistribution rights; reviewed lexicon; better content authoring; broader platform testing. Any enrollment, student records, automated assessment or campus networking feature requires its own privacy and operational design. Autonomous agents, health monitoring and decisions about children are outside this release.
